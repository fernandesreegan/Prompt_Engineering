## 💧 Hydration

Aim for approximately **2–2.5 litres of water per day**, adjusting according to weather, exercise, and sweating.

Prefer:

- Water
- Unsweetened buttermilk
- Unsweetened herbal drinks

Limit sugary beverages and packaged juices.

---

## 🍽️ Portion Control

1. Make vegetables roughly **half of the main lunch/dinner plate**.
2. Keep cooked rice or millet to around **1 cup per meal**.
3. Eat slowly and stop when comfortably satisfied rather than completely full.

---

## 🚫 Foods to Limit

Try to consume these less frequently:

- Deep-fried snacks
- Sweets and desserts
- Sugary drinks
- Packaged fruit juices
- Bakery products
- Refined-flour foods
- Large portions of white rice
- Excess oil or ghee
- Frequent restaurant or fast-food meals

---

## 🏃 Healthy Habits

1. Maintain regular meal timings and avoid unnecessary late-night snacking.
2. Include regular physical activity such as **brisk walking** alongside normal daily activity.
3. Prepare vegetables, dosa/idli batter, sprouts, and cooked legumes ahead of time to keep weekday cooking within **30 minutes**.
