## 👤 C — CONTEXT & CONSTRAINTS

The meal plan must be recognisably **South Indian and vegetarian**.

Prefer familiar dishes and ingredients such as:

**Idli, dosa, adai, upma, pongal, ragi, millet, rice, sambar, rasam, kootu, poriyal, curd, sundal, sprouts, lentils, vegetables and seasonal fruits.**

### Prioritize

- Seasonal and locally available vegetables and fruits
- Whole grains and millets
- Dal, legumes, sprouts and other vegetarian protein sources
- Fibre-rich foods
- Moderate quantities of rice and other carbohydrates
- Minimal added sugar
- Limited oil
- Simple weekday preparation
- Meals that can reasonably be shared with the household

### Avoid

- Crash diets or extreme calorie restriction
- Skipping meals as a weight-loss strategy
- Excessive fried foods, sweets and sugary drinks
- Highly processed foods
- Unnecessarily expensive or imported ingredients
- Building the plan around foods such as **quinoa, avocado or other ingredients that are not typical everyday South Indian staples**
- Complicated recipes that exceed the stated weekday cooking time

Adapt the plan to the stated allergies, restrictions and dislikes.

If seasonal information is provided, prefer produce commonly available during that season.

Do not repeat the same main meal on consecutive days.
